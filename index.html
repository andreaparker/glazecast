IIIEvidence
—Method

Recipes are reduced to oxide moles and a unity formula. Multivalent cations are speciated against an atmosphere ladder. Each candidate phase gets a saturation ratio, a liquidus depression and a time–temperature–transformation curve, and the kiln schedule is integrated against those curves. Crystals and dopants feed a Fresnel / ligand-field colour model. Structures and habits are real crystallography. The kinetic constants are calibrated against known glazes, not derived from first principles. Absolute times are good to ±half a decade. The ranking, the mechanisms and the levers are the reliable output.
