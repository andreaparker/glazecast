33,155 published glazes. Same chemistry, repeated.

Every public glaze recipe in the Glazy archive, reduced to oxide moles and run through the same model as the tool on the front page.

No two of these recipes were made by the same person using the same bag of feldspar, and the archive itself is noisy: duplicates, variants, recipes shared before they were tested. What is not noisy is the oxide chemistry underneath. Each row below is one recipe, its oxide analysis as published, and what that analysis says will crystallise on the surface when fired. A row that predicts a crystal is a recipe where the chemistry sits above the phase's saturation threshold; it still needs the right cooling. The column Si:Al is the silica-to-alumina molar ratio, the single number most glaze chemistry reduces to.
